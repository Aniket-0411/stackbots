# World 1 "Boot Up" — intended solutions (designer-1)

Bots: 1 Coral, 2 Teal, 3 Marigold, 4 Lilac. "Tower of N" = N bots stacked; the top bot can climb 2 + (N-1) tiles.
For a 4-high tower, two pairs stack: pair A+B stands still, pair C+D (D on C's head) jumps together onto B.

## 1-1 Power On (min 2)
- Anyone walks right, hops the 1-high bump, grabs the Power Cell, hops the 2-high block and enters the hatch (Up). Everyone follows.
- Gears: one on top of the 2-high block (solo hop); one on the 3-high pillar past the hatch (one bot stands still, a buddy jumps on its head, then onto the pillar).

## 1-2 Mind the Gap (min 2)
- Walk right jumping gaps of 1, 2 (pits), climb the 2-high plateau, jump the 3-wide pit to the second plateau, take the cell, hop down, jump the last 2-wide pit, enter.
- Gears: one hangs over the 3-wide pit (grab it mid-jump); one on the 3-high pillar behind the hatch (piggyback).

## 1-3 Piggyback (min 2)
- Bot 1 stands at the foot of the 3-high pillar on the left; bot 2 jumps on its head, then onto the pillar and takes the cell.
- Everyone goes right over the 2-high block and the 2-wide pit to the hatch.
- Gears: on the 4-high back post (hop up one more from the cell pillar); on the 4-high block at the far right (tower of 3).

## 1-4 Pointy End (min 2)
- Jump the 1-, 2- and 3-wide spike strips, climb the 2-high plateau for the cell, jump the 3-wide spike trench to the next plateau, hop the last spike, enter.
- Gears: over the 3-wide spike strip (mid-jump); on the 3-high pillar at the right edge (piggyback).

## 1-5 Totem Pole (min 3)
- Bots 1, 2, 3 build a tower of 3 right beside the floating shelf (4 up); the top bot steps onto the shelf for the cell and drops back down.
- Everyone jumps the 2-wide pit and climbs the 2-high step to the hatch.
- Gears: one on the cell shelf; one on the higher shelf (5 up) needs all four as a tower.

## 1-6 Skylight (min 3)
- The corridor ceiling is too low for towers: build the tower of 3 directly under the 3-wide skylight; the top bot pops up onto the upper floor.
- Upstairs: walk right for the cell, then drop back down through the skylight and carry it to the hatch.
- Gears: two in the upper room (one needs a solo hop onto the 2-high block); one on the 3-high pillar by the hatch (piggyback).

## 1-7 Bot Ladder (min 4)
- Three terraces, each 3 high. Bot 1 stays at the foot of terrace 1 as a step; bots 2, 3, 4 climb over it onto terrace 1.
- On terrace 1 bot 2 is the step; bots 3, 4 climb to terrace 2. On terrace 2 bot 3 is the step; bot 4 climbs to the top and takes the cell.
- Bot 4 drops off the right side; the others drop back down and walk through the tunnel under the terraces to the hatch.
- Gears: one 2 tiles above the summit (jump); one on the 3-high pillar right of the hatch (piggyback).

## 1-8 All Hands Up (min 4)
- The cell shelf is 5 up: all four stack beside it (bots 1+2 as a pair, bots 3+4 hop together onto them); the top bot grabs the cell.
- Hop the 2-high bump and enter the hatch.
- Gears: one on the cell shelf; one on the 5-high pillar at the far right (another 4-bot tower).

## 1-9 Spike Island (min 4)
- Jump island to island over the spike sea. On the 4-wide island right of the shelf's left end (or the 3-wide island on its right) build the 4-bot tower; the top bot takes the cell and steps back down onto an island.
- Hop the remaining islands to the goal platform and enter.
- Gears: one above the last spike strip (mid-jump); one on the 4-high post at the far right (tower of 3).

## 1-10 Stack Overflow (min 4)
- Terrace 1 is 4 high: bots 1+2+3 build a tower at its foot and bot 3 climbs up. Bot 4 then jumps on the leftover pair (1+2) and climbs up too.
- On terrace 1, bot 3 is the step; bot 4 climbs terrace 2 (3 high), jumps the 3-wide spike pit on top and takes the cell, then drops off the right side.
- Bots 1 and 2 walk through the low tunnel, bot 3 drops back down; everyone jumps the 3-wide pit and climbs the 2-high hatch deck.
- Gears: one above the summit spike pit (mid-jump); one on the 5-high post at spawn (4-bot tower); one on the 4-high post on the hatch deck (tower of 3).
