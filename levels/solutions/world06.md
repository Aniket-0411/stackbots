# World 6 "Bounce House" — intended solutions

Physics notes, measured in the engine:
- A lone spring bounce lands on ledges up to 5 tiles above the spring's floor.
- A 2-bot stack walking onto a spring launches together, and the top bot lands on a 6-high ledge.
- Bots walking against a belt move at about 1.2 px/tick. A crate cannot be pushed upstream.

**6-1 Boing!** (2p): Each bot bounces on the spring at col 8 over the 5-high wall. Anyone bounces at col 20 onto the cell shelf. Everyone bounces at col 28 onto the hatch plateau. Gears: a 2-stack on the col-15 spring for the 6-high ledge, and a 2-tower on the plateau.

**6-2 Half-Baked Bridge** (2p): Cross the crumble bridges alone or in tight pairs, and wait for the tiles to respawn between groups. Grab the cell on the island. Hop onto the crumble step at cols 32-33 and jump straight up onto the hatch ledge before the step drops.

**6-3 Walkalator** (2p): Two bots push the need-2 crate right onto the `>` belt. It rides into the spike slot at col 20 and becomes a stepping stone. Everyone jumps from the crate over the remaining 3-wide spikes, then trudges against the `<` belt to the hatch. Verified in the sim.

**6-4 Piggyback Pogo** (2p): One bot rides on another's head, and the bottom bot walks onto the spring at col 14. The top bot lands on the 6-high slab. The others walk through the tunnel, take the cell at col 21, and drop onto the lift in the pit at cols 26-28. The flyer flips the lever at col 30 and the lift carries them up. Verified in the sim.

**6-5 Trapdoor Delivery** (2p): Push the need-2 crate onto the crumble trapdoor at cols 12-15. It falls to the lower floor, and the pushers fall with it. Pick up the cell at col 8. Push the crate right onto the hold plate at col 24, which keeps gate A open, and everyone enters the hatch room. Crate drop verified in the sim.

**6-6 Treadmill Duty** (2p): Every plate sits at the upstream end of a belt, so whoever holds it has to keep walking into the wall or the bump. Coral holds A on the left belt. Someone inside holds the second A plate (col 23) so Coral can come through. Then someone holds B (col 25, leaning on the bump), the cell carrier goes through gate B, and the plate inside the hatch room (col 35) holds gate B open for the last bot.

**6-7 Pop Goes the Bot** (2p): Someone holds plate A at col 19 downstairs. That opens the hole over the left springs, and the others bounce up. Upstairs, someone holds plate B at col 21, which opens the right hole, and the plate holder bounces up through it at cols 27-28. Take the cell at col 4, then go to the hatch. Verified in the sim.

**6-8 Conga Crumble** (2p): All bots stand on the 4-wide timer plate (need 4). The gate at col 29 then stays open for 540 ticks after the first bot steps off. The crumble bridge drops a loose single file, so the team crosses as two tight 2-stacks or one tower. Take the cell, then go to the hatch. Gear: a 2-stack on the spring at col 37. Verified in the sim with two pairs.

**6-9 Baggage Claim** (2p): Gate A needs both plates held, so crates must hold them. Two bots push the need-2 crate left onto the `<` belt, which pins it on plate A at col 2. Another bot bounces on the spring at col 18 onto the shelf and takes the cell. That bot shoves the crate off the shelf's end; it lands on the `>` belt, which pins it on plate B at col 29. Everyone enters the hatch room. Verified in the sim.

**6-10 Bounce House** (4p):
1. A 2-stack pogo puts one bot on the slab. The rest pick up the cell in the tunnel (col 18) and stand on the pit lift at cols 21-23. The flyer flips the lever at col 16.
2. All four stand on the need-4 timer plate (cols 24-27). Then they cross the crumble bridge as two tight 2-stacks before gate B (col 45) closes.
3. All four push the need-4 crate onto the `>` belt, which parks it against the 3-high hatch ledge. Climb the crate, then the ledge.

The full run was scripted and cleared in the sim with 0 deaths.
