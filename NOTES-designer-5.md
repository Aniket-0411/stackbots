# NOTES: designer 5 (worlds 9 "Hazard Zone" and 10 "Summit")

## Files
- `levels/world09.json` and `levels/world10.json` are GENERATED by `tools/designer-5/world09.js` and `world10.js` (run `node tools/designer-5/world10.js [--print]`). Edit the builders, not the JSON.
- `tools/designer-5/lib.js`: grid builder and world formatter. `drive.js`: closed-loop bot driver on top of `src/engine.js` (steps: to, onto, leap, hold, wait, until, up).
- Solutions: `levels/solutions/world09.md`, `world10.md`.

## Decisions
- **Laser emitters sit inside solid tiles** (wall- or ceiling-mounted), never in empty notches. The engine traces the beam from the emitter tile's edge whatever that tile holds. An empty notch let crates and bots slide into the emitter and switch off its shield. Exceptions: lane/track emitters that hang inside a corridor's top row (9-9, 10-5, 10-9).
- **Laser `logic: "any"`** is used in 10-4 (SPEC section 6: every target reads its channel with its logic, and the engine supports it). `tools/validate-levels.js` warns "unknown field logic" for lasers. That is a validator gap (QA).
- Cell-in-the-beam (WARN laser-spot) is deliberate in 9-7, 10-2, 10-3 and 10-10: the cell is collected from behind a crate shield.
- 10-5 colour walls c/d with minPlayers 2 (WARN) is deliberate: the lanes are independent, so an absent bot's lane simply stays closed.
- 10-3 HEUR "stranded": the heuristic cannot model the crate dropping through the crumble trapdoor. The sim confirms the crate drops, is pushed to col 39 and becomes the exit step.
- 10-10 stage 1 needs a 4-bot totem. A 4th bot cannot jump 90 px onto a 3-stack, so the totem is built with a mid-air swap: the top bot hops and the 4th slips onto the stack under it. Sim-verified.

## Engine notes
- A crate sliding sideways onto a spring is not launched (the spring surface is 20 px into the tile), so no "crate cannon" designs.
- A crate pushed across a 1-tile crumble never falls (the tile vanishes 30 ticks late, when the crate already rests partly on solid ground). Hence the stopper block in 10-3.
