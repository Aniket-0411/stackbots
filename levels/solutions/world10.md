# World 10 "Summit": intended solutions

Bots: Coral = 1 (slot 0), Teal = 2, Marigold = 3, Lilac = 4. Coordinates are [col,row].

## 10-1 Base Camp
2-stack under the floating shelf (cols 7-9). The top bot grabs the cell (9,12) and hits latch A (7,12), which removes the lid gate over the spring (20,15).
Everyone walks onto the spring and pops up the 5-high cliff. Two bots push the crate (28,10) right against the 3-high wall (col 36) as a step. Hatch at (44,7).

## 10-2 Box Fort Ferry
Riders drop onto the 3-wide ferry (8,12) behind the 2-tall crate. The always-on beam from the far wall (27,11) hits the crate, not them. The operator pulls lever A (6,10) and hops on (or stacks on a head).
The riders pass through the cell (16,11) in the shielded row, then climb crate (2) onto the far ledge. Hatch at (44,9). Crate need 4, so riders can't push the shield off by accident.

## 10-3 Trapdoor Freight
Two bots push the crate right until the stopper (13,11) stops it on the cracked tile (12,12). It drops into the corridor and blocks the beam fired from the far end (40,14).
Drop into the hole after it. Two bots push the crate right through the corridor while the rest walk behind it, picking up the cell (30,14). The crate stops at col 39 as the step out (2 up to the deck). Hatch at (44,11).

## 10-4 Stand-In
One bot stands on plate A on the plinth (10,18), which turns off the shaft beam (24,13). The rest push the crate onto the need-4 lift (20,20) and ride up with it: the crate counts as the 4th rider.
Up top, push the crate off onto plate A2 (27,7), which keeps the beam off. Riders drop back down the shaft so the lift has 4 riders again (holder included), then all ride up. Cell at (13,17), hatch at (42,7).

## 10-5 Lane Change
Climb the lobby's one-way ladder. Each bot enters its own coloured lane: Coral (row 6) dodges bobbing saws, Teal (row 10) times blinking beams, Marigold (row 14) walks against a belt and hops bumps under a patrolling ceiling saw, Lilac (row 18) keeps moving across a crumble bridge over spikes.
All drop into the shaft onto the need-4 lift (42,23). A rider in col 43 takes the cell (43,13) on the way up. Hatch at (48,3). An absent bot's lane just stays closed.

## 10-6 Special Delivery
2-stack beside the floating shelf (cols 3-6). The top bot takes the cell (3,14) and pulls lever A (5,14). The hopper floor opens and the 2-tall crate drops onto the belt (11,18).
The belt carries the crate under the bobbing saws to the cliff face (col 35). Everyone rides the belt (dodging the saws) and climbs crate (2) then 2 more onto the cliff. Hatch at (43,13).

## 10-7 Pinball Peak
Each spring has a lid, open while either plate of its letter is held. Ground: X holds A (8,23); the others spring up through the one-way hatch (16-18,19); one holds A (24,18); X follows.
Repeat each floor: B (10,18) / B (22,13); C (14,13) / C (20,8); E (12,8) / E (6,3). Bobbing saws on floors 2 and 3. Cell (26,8), hatch (14,3).

## 10-8 Call the Lift
Riders on the lever lift (18,24). Operator at lever A (12,23) switches it on when the riders shout "GO" (straight-ride window about 75 ticks; flip off to back down if late). Riders pass the blinking beams (rows 17, 11) and the cell (20,14).
At the top a rider switches the top lever (25,4) off. The lift comes down, the operator boards, and the top bot switches it on and calls the timing for them. Hatch at (36,4).

## 10-9 Two-Track Mind
2+ bots ride the need-2 lift (9,19) to the upper track; the rest walk the lower track. Upper holds A (15,8), which turns off the lower beam door (col 18).
Lower holds B (27,18), which raises the drawbridge over the upper chasm (cols 24-29). Upper holds C (33,8), which opens the lower gate (col 36). Lower run across the crumble (cols 40-44); upper dodge two bobbing saws, take the cell (46,8) and drop down at col 52. Hatch at (61,18).

## 10-10 Top of the World (needs 4)
(1) Build a 4-bot totem under the one-way walkway (row 19). Two pairs: one pair stands as a 2-stack, the other 2-stack hops onto its heads (pair-on-pair) — no mid-air swap needed (QA sim-proven). The top bot jumps up through the walkway and hits latch A (17,18), which opens the closet gate (col 7). The other three spring up from (3,23).
(2) All four push the need-4 crate (19,18) along B1 toward the beam (35,18), sheltering behind it and collecting the cell (27,18). The crate stops at col 34 as the step onto B2.
(3) Squeeze into the saw-lined need-4 lift (44,16) in cols 45-47 (three side by side plus one on a head) and ride to the summit. Hatch at (62,5).
