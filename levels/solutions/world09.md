# World 9 "Hazard Zone": intended solutions

Bots: Coral = 1 (slot 0), Teal = 2, Marigold = 3, Lilac = 4. "2-stack" means one bot stands on another's head.

## 9-1 Buzz Off
Jump the 2-wide pit (cols 8-9) where the static blade sits. Walk under the rising blade at col 15 while it is up (rows 9-13).
Two bots stack under the shelf (cols 22-27, 3 high). The top bot grabs the cell at (25,10), drops down, and everyone walks right to the hatch (37,13).

## 9-2 Blink Tunnel
Four ceiling beams (cols 13/18/23/28) blink in a rolling wave (90 ticks on, 90 off, each 45 ticks behind the last).
Watch the wave, then move together in its wake. Grab the cell at (20,13) on the way and run to the hatch (42,13). The gear at col 23 sits in a beam column.

## 9-3 Elevator Etiquette
The cell is on a 2-high box (11,15): any bot jumps up for it. All 4 board the 5-wide need-4 elevator in the shaft (cols 20-24) and it rises to row 4.
The blades at (20,13) and (24,8) cover the outer columns, so the riders squeeze into cols 21-23. With 4 bots, someone rides on a head (or form two 2-stacks).
At the top, step right onto the ledge and walk to the hatch (29,3). Elevator need clamps to the bot count.

## 9-4 Treadmill Duty
The gate (col 22) opens while either button A is held (any logic). Both buttons sit on conveyors that drift the holder toward a corner saw, so the holder must keep walking into the belt.
Bot X holds the left button (cols 6-7) while the others pass the gate. One of them holds the right button (cols 36-37, belt drifts right) so X can come through.
Two bots stack under the shelf (cols 28-31) for the cell at (30,10), then deliver it to the hatch (25,13).

## 9-5 Blink Lift
Drop into the pit and grab the cell (11,13). A laser fires from the plateau face (8,12) across the pit at a 2-stack's head height (row 12), 120 ticks on and 120 off.
A 2-stack at the right wall (col 20) climbs the 3-high wall onto the lift (18,11) or the ledge, timing the top bot's jump to the OFF window. That bot holds button A (25,10), which raises the lift (18,14 to 18,11).
The others step onto the lift while it is down (row 14 is under the beam) and ride up when the button is held. A single bot walking upright stays under the beam.

## 9-6 Piggyback Express
All 4 stand on the need-4 timer plate (cols 5-8): the beam at the door (col 20) switches off for 300 ticks after they step off.
The crumble bridge (cols 10-17) over spikes vanishes 30 ticks after it is touched, so bots cannot cross single file. They form two 2-stacks (or ride piggyback) and dash across. Riders don't touch the bridge, so fewer tiles get triggered.
Pass the door before the beam returns, grab the cell (26,12), and go to the hatch (37,12). If the bridge falls, it reappears after 180 ticks, then retry.

## 9-7 Crate Convoy
An always-on floor laser fires left from (42,13) along the corridor floor row. The 2-tall crate (need 2) blocks it.
Two bots push the crate right along the corridor while the others walk behind it in its shadow. Dodge the three bobbing ceiling blades (cols 15, 23, 31) by waiting at the low point.
The cell at (20,13) is picked up from behind the crate. At the end the crate rests against the goal ledge (col 41) as a step; the 3-high ledge needs crate (2) plus a jump. Hatch at (45,10).

## 9-8 Flip Flop
Six ceiling beams on channel A (cols 13, 17, 21, 25, 29, 33). Cols 13/21/29 fire while A is off; cols 17/25/33 fire while A is on. A = near lever (8,13, starts off) AND far lever (39,13, starts on).
The operator stays at the near lever and flips on the runners' "FLIP!". Each flip opens the beam in front of the runners, who hop pocket to pocket (3-wide pockets) and grab the cell at (23,13).
To bring the operator over: the operator leaves the near lever ON and walks in. A runner at the far lever now controls A (on = A active, off = inactive) and flips for the operator the same way. Hatch at (45,13).

## 9-9 Leapfrog Lanes
Two lanes, upper (rows 7-9) and lower (rows 11-13). Each door is opened by a hold plate in the OTHER lane, just before that door's position.
Two bots go up — the upper lane is 3 tiles above the step at (6,13), so a lower-lane bot gives each one a piggyback — and two stay low. They leapfrog: low bot holds plate A (11,13) so the upper door at 13 opens; an upper bot holds plate B (16,9) so the lower door at 18 opens; and so on.
Lower lane: three ceiling beams blink (90/90). Upper lane: two bobbing blades. The upper team takes the cell (39,9), then everyone drops to the hatch (46,13).

## 9-10 Hazard Pay (needs 3)
Stage 1: the cliff (row 16) is 4 tiles high, so a 3-bot tower climbs at col 15. A blade sweeps along row 16 (cols 8-15) just above the tower's top bot. Wait for it to pass, then the top bot jumps. The climber lands on latch button A (17,15) and opens the tunnel gate (16,18).
Stage 2: the rest walk through the tunnel onto the need-3 lift (18,20) and ride up through the blinking beam (21,17), boarding while it is OFF (150 ticks).
Stage 3: everyone stands on the need-4 timer plate (25-28). The laser door at col 43 stays off while the timer runs (540 ticks after they step off). Grab the cell (35,15) and run the twin beams (cols 32, 37, which blink together 70 on / 150 off). Hatch at (52,15).
