# World 5 "Prism" — intended solutions

Bots: Coral = slot 0 (`a`), Teal = slot 1 (`b`), Marigold = slot 2 (`c`), Lilac = slot 3 (`d`).

**5-1 Paint by Bots** (4p): Each bot walks into the hut with its own colored door: Marigold col 7, Coral col 13, Lilac col 19, Teal col 25. Each one latches the plate inside. When all four are latched, gate A (col 34) opens. Anyone takes the cell to the hatch.

**5-2 Piggyback Bridge** (2p): Coral falls through `a` and Teal falls through `b`. Teal carries Coral over the `a` span to the pillar. There they swap: Coral steps onto the pillar, Teal hops onto her head, and Coral carries Teal over the `b` span. Marigold and Lilac just walk across. Verified with 2 bots in the sim.

**5-3 Teal's Secret Tunnel** (2p): Everyone starts penned in. Teal drops through the `b` floor (cols 5-6), grabs the cell in the tunnel, and climbs out at cols 15-16. Teal then climbs the steps and latches the plate at (24,10). The pen gate opens, and everyone goes to the hatch.

**5-4 Who's on Top?** (3p): Build a 3-tower in the nook at cols 17-18. Teal can't be the base because the floor there is `b`. Marigold must be on top. Marigold goes into the nook first, then Lilac or Teal hops onto the base, then Marigold hops on. Marigold jumps up through the `cc` ceiling, steps onto the upper floor and latches (10,10). That opens gate A, and the others climb the 2-high step at cols 6-7. Verified in the sim.

**5-5 Hold That Hue** (4p): Each bot climbs the one-way ladder to its own lane: Coral top, Teal 2nd, Marigold 3rd, Lilac bottom. The plates form a chain. Coral holds A, which opens Teal's gate. Teal holds B, which opens Marigold's gate. Marigold holds C, which opens Lilac's gate. Lilac holds D (she grabs the cell on the way), which opens Coral's exit gate at col 20. Coral leaves first, then everyone exits right.

**5-6 Courier Code** (4p): Boost Marigold with a 2-tower so she can reach the `c` vault (3 high) for the cell. Lilac drops through the `dd` floor into the cellar and holds the plate, which opens gate A. Everyone else passes. Someone holds the plate at (31,14) so Lilac can climb out and follow.

**5-7 Totem Pole** (4p): Teal fetches the cell from the `b` closet. The team builds a 4-tower at col 17 with Lilac on top. Only she can pass the `d` lip onto the upper floor, 5 high. Lilac latches (24,9), which opens gate A. The other three walk under the upper floor and ride the need-3 lift at cols 29-31 up to the hatch.

**5-8 Line Up!** (4p): The need-4 lift rises through the `bbddaacc` stripes. It only moves if every bot stands under its own color: Teal, Lilac, Coral, Marigold from left to right. Players reorder by hopping over each other on the lift. The sim confirms it rises in the right order and stalls in the wrong one.

**5-9 Countdown Doors** (4p): The sealed room has exits 5 high (`a`), 4 high (`b`), 2 high (`c`) and 1 high (`d`). Coral leaves first on a 4-tower, then Teal on a 3-tower made of Marigold and Lilac. Then Marigold and Lilac step out alone. Both sides climb the one-way ladders to the roof hatch. Someone carries the cell from inside the room.

**5-10 Prism Break** (4p):
1. Lilac fetches the cell from her `d` closet.
2. A 4-tower with Coral on top gets her through the `a` window. She latches the plate, which opens gate A.
3. The trapdoor bridge (`aaaabbbbccccdddd`) is crossed in pairs. Teal carries Coral over `a`, then they swap at the a/b seam and Coral carries Teal the rest of the way. Lilac carries Marigold over `a`, `b` and `c`, then they swap at the c/d seam. Both pairs verified in the sim.
4. Each bot drops into its own colored cellar onto a timer plate (channel B, all). When all four are on, gate B opens and everyone rushes to the hatch.
