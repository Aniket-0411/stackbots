# World 2 "Heavy Lifting" — intended solutions (designer-1)

Bots: 1 Coral, 2 Teal, 3 Marigold, 4 Lilac. Crates are only pushed (never pulled); a crate pushed past a ledge falls straight down.
2-7 and 2-8 were replayed in the real engine with 2 bots (`node tools/designer-1/prove-w2.js`).

## 2-1 Box Step (min 2)
- Push the crate right to the 3-high plateau; climb crate (1) then plateau (2). Take the cell, drop down to the hatch.
- Gears: one above the crate's path (jump from the crate); one on the floating perch (piggyback on the plateau).

## 2-2 Heave Ho (min 2)
- The 2x2 crate needs two pushers (a chain counts). Push it to the 4-high wall: crate (2) + wall (2). Cell on top, hatch below.
- Gears: above the crate's start (jump from it); on the 4-high post right of the hatch (tower of 3).

## 2-3 Pit Crew (min 2)
- Push both floor crates into the 5-wide spike trench (the second slides over the first): 3-wide gap left, jump it.
- Climb the 2-high block, grab the cell, push the crate on the block right: it drops into the 4-wide trench at its foot. Jump the rest, enter.
- Gears: over the first trench (jump); on the 3-high post (piggyback).

## 2-4 Return to Sender (min 2)
- Piggyback one bot onto terrace 1; it hops over the crate and shoves it LEFT off the edge: a step for everyone.
- Same again on terrace 2 (piggyback, hop over the crate, push it off onto terrace 1). Cell and hatch on top.
- Gears: above terrace 1 (piggyback jump); on the 3-high pillar by the hatch (piggyback).

## 2-5 Heavy Metal (min 2)
- The 4x2 crate needs everybody. Push it into the 4-wide spike pit: it fits exactly and becomes a bridge, 1 below the far lip.
- Piggyback to the cell shelf, enter the hatch.
- Gears: above the big crate's start (jump from it); on the 5-high post (four-bot tower).

## 2-6 Conga Line (min 2)
- Inside the 1-tall tunnel nobody can stack: three bots push the crate in a line (need 3, clamps to the team size), grabbing the cell on the way.
- Push it on into the 4-wide spike trench; jump the remaining 3. Enter.
- Gears: in the nook in the tunnel ceiling (hop up); on the 4-high post (tower of 3).

## 2-7 Double Decker (min 2)
- Two bots push crate A (needs 2) to the 4-high wall. Piggyback on crate A: the top bot climbs the wall.
- Up top it hops over crate B and pushes it left off the edge: B lands on A. A + B + wall = 1 + 1 + 2 steps for everyone.
- Gears: on the little shelf (piggyback); on the 4-high pillar past the hatch (tower of 3).

## 2-8 Pothole Patrol (min 2)
- Order matters: first push the little crate through the crawl tunnel and into the 2-wide pothole at the cliff foot.
- Then two bots push the big crate off the shelf, walk back through the tunnel and shove it to the cliff: it rides over the little crate and stops at full height.
- Ground -> big crate (2) -> cliff (2). (Big crate first = it sinks into the pothole and the cliff is 3 too high.)
- Gears: above the big crate (jump from it); on the 3-high pillar past the hatch (piggyback).

## 2-9 Tall Order (min 4)
- Two bots push crate A (3 wide) under the ledge until the floor bump stops it.
- From A, climb onto the ledge and push crate B off its end: it lands exactly on A. A + B = a 2-high step.
- All four stack on B (pair onto pair); the top bot steps onto the cell shelf (7 up from the floor) and drops back down.
- Gears: above the ledge (piggyback); on the 4-high post past the hatch (tower of 3).

## 2-10 Moving Day (min 4)
- Three bots push the tunnel crate through the mountain and into the spike trench; jump the remaining 3 tiles.
- All four shove the fridge (needs 4) to the 5-high wall. Piggyback on the fridge: the top bot climbs up, hops over the box and pushes it off onto the fridge.
- Fridge (2) + box (1) + wall (2): everyone climbs. Four-bot tower beside the pillar for the cell; drop down, enter the hatch.
- Gears: in the tunnel nook (hop up); above the trench (jump); next to the cell on the pillar (four-bot tower).
