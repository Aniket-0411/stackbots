How to play

Carry the Power Cell into the Exit Hatch to unlock it, then every bot climbs in. Bots are solid: stand on each other's heads, push heavy crates together, hold buttons for friends. If anyone gets scrapped, the whole team restarts.

Online: ←→ or AD move · Space Z W ↑ jump · W/↑ also enter hatch / flip lever · ↓+jump drops through thin floors.

Local: P1 WASD (+Space) · P2 arrows (+Enter) · P3/P4 gamepads (A jump, Y / d-pad up interact). Tab hands P1's keys to the next idle bot.

R restart · Esc pause · M mute
